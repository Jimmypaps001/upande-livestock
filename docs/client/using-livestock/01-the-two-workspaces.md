# The two workspaces

Open Desk and find **Upande Livestock** in the app sidebar (the paw-print
icon). It gives you two workspaces.

| Workspace | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Look at the herd — headline numbers, milk trend, herd tiles |
| **Operations** | Record what happened — every activity, one tab each |

Home is for reading. Operations is for writing. If you are entering something
that happened today, you want Operations.

## Home

Home shows a single dashboard with four headline figures across the top:

| Figure | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Active Animals** | Every animal still on the farm — animals that died, were sold, culled or disposed of are excluded |
| **Milk Production** | The net yield totalled across the most recent day that has any milk recorded, with that date shown beneath it |
| **Health Events** | Health cases opened in the last 7 days |
| **Births** | Calving and birth events recorded since the 1st of the current month |

Below that:

- **Milk yield, last 30 days** — a daily line of net yield.
- **Top Herds** — the five herds with the highest net yield over the last 30
  days.
- **Herd tiles** — up to twelve herds, each showing head count, how many are
  milking, how many are pregnant, and the average yield per cow at that herd's
  most recent milking.

> **Known quirk — herd tile head counts.** The head count on a *herd tile*
> counts every animal whose herd is set to that herd, including animals that
> have since died or been sold. The **Active Animals** figure at the top, the
> animal dropdowns in Operations, and the head count feeding uses all exclude
> retired animals. So a herd tile can read higher than the herd really is.
> Trust the Animals list or the feeding screen for a true count.

Home also carries shortcut buttons straight to the **Animals**, **Herds**,
**Milk Recording**, **Health Cases** and **Livestock Events** lists.

## Operations

Operations is a single screen with thirteen tabs — one per kind of activity.
It is the main data-entry surface of the app, and [the next
chapter](02-the-operations-screen.md) walks through it.

## The sidebar

Underneath the two workspaces, the sidebar lists the records themselves:

| Group | Entry | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Animals & Herds** | Animals | The Animal list |
| | Herds | The Herds list |
| **Production** | Milk Recording | The Milk Recording list |
| **Livestock Events** | Livestock Events | Every recorded activity |
| | Health Cases | Animals under treatment |
| | Diagnoses | Check-ups and pregnancy diagnoses |
| | Diseases | The disease reference list |

You can record through these list views directly if you prefer standard Desk
forms — Operations is a faster front door to the same records, not a separate
system. Anything booked in Operations shows up in these lists immediately.
